CURRENT SCOPE

Keep what matters.

EverSave reads unencrypted PC BND4 saves, verifies checksums, and makes full-file backups and portable character archives. A valid checksum does not prove that the game will accept a save. Restoration generates a new .sl2 copy for testing. Game compatibility has not yet been verified; .erchar files themselves are not playable .sl2 saves.
