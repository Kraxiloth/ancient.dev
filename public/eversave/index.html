CURRENT SCOPE

Keep what matters.

EverSave reads unencrypted PC BND4 saves, verifies checksums, and makes full-file backups and portable character archives. A valid checksum does not prove that the game will accept a save. Restoration generates a new .sl2 copy for testing. A restored save was recognized by Elden Ring 1.17 in one user test; .erchar files themselves are not playable .sl2 saves.
